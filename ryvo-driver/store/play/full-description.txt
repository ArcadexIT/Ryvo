Ryvo Driver is the app for roadside assistance professionals who help drivers with emergency car jump-starts. Go online, accept nearby jobs, navigate to the customer, complete the service, and get paid — all in one place.

GO ONLINE & EARN
Toggle online when you’re ready to work. Receive nearby jump-start requests with distance, payout, and job details so you can decide quickly.

ACCEPT JOBS INSTANTLY
Review incoming requests and accept or decline with one tap. Stay in control of your schedule while helping stranded drivers.

NAVIGATE WITH CONFIDENCE
Open turn-by-turn navigation to the customer’s location, mark arrival, start the job, and update status until completion.

GET PAID FAST
Track earnings, manage your wallet, add bank details, and withdraw your payouts. Clear history helps you see every completed job.

VERIFIED PROVIDER PROFILE
Complete identity and document verification (licence, vehicle registration, insurance, selfie, and equipment) so customers can trust who is arriving.

STAY CONNECTED
Chat with customers during active jobs, receive push notifications for new requests, and manage your profile, vehicle info, and performance.

WHO IT’S FOR
Independent roadside assistance providers and operators legally authorised to offer jump-start and related vehicle support services.
