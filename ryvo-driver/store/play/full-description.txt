Ryvo Driver is the app for independent drivers who take passenger trips on Ryvo. Go online, accept nearby ride requests, navigate to the pickup, complete the trip, and withdraw your earnings.

GO ONLINE
Toggle online when you are ready to work. Ryvo matches you with nearby ride requests in your service area. A notification stays visible while your location is shared, and you can go offline at any time.

ACCEPT OR DECLINE
Each request shows pickup, dropoff, and your earning. Accept or decline before the offer expires. You stay in control of which trips you take.

NAVIGATE THE TRIP
Follow the trip on the map, open Google Maps for driving directions, mark arrival, and complete the ride. Riders can see that you are on the way.

GET PAID
Track earnings, add a bank account or Stripe payouts, and withdraw. Every completed trip stays in your history with the platform fee and your net payout.

VERIFIED DRIVERS
Create an account, accept the driver agreement, and submit your identity, driving licence, vehicle, and insurance. Riders only meet drivers who have passed verification.

TRIP CHAT
Message the rider during an active trip. You can report a conversation to Ryvo support from the chat screen.

WHO IT IS FOR
Independent drivers who are legally allowed to carry passengers in the United Kingdom. Ryvo Driver is not the rider app. You must be 18 or older.
