- Initial release.
- Customer app: ride, bid, and parcel booking, live tracking, chat, wallet, card and cash payment, invoices, support.
- Driver app: go online, accept trips, navigation, KYC, earnings, Stripe payouts, subscriptions.
- Admin panel: dispatch, live map, riders, drivers, verification, pricing, finance, content, staff roles.
- API: NestJS, PostgreSQL, Redis, Socket.IO.
