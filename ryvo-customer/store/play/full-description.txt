Ryvo is the rider app for booking a car, sending a parcel, or offering your own fare. Set a pickup and drop-off, see the price before you confirm, and follow the driver on the map until you arrive.

BOOK A RIDE
Choose a vehicle, confirm the route, and send the request. Nearby drivers can accept the trip or respond to your offer.

SEND A PARCEL
Use the same flow to send a parcel. You get a driver, a live map, and a record of the job.

LIVE TRIP
Watch the driver move on the map, chat in the app, and share the trip with someone you trust. When the trip ends, rate the driver.

PAY IN THE APP
Pay by card through Stripe, with your Ryvo wallet, or in cash where the trip allows it. Ryvo does not store your full card number. Receipts and past trips stay in the app.

YOUR ACCOUNT
Save places you use often, manage payment methods, and reach in-app support if a trip needs help. You can request account deletion from Profile.

Ryvo is for riders aged 18 and over, where the service operates. Drivers use the separate Ryvo Driver app.
