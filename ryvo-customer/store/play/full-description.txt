Ryvo gets you emergency car jump-start help when your battery dies. Request a nearby verified provider, track their arrival live, and pay securely in the app.

INSTANT JUMP START
Open the app, confirm your location, and request help in a few taps. Nearby providers are matched so help can reach you quickly.

VERIFIED & TRUSTED
Providers complete identity and document checks so you know a real roadside professional is on the way.

LIVE TRACKING
Follow your provider on the map with real-time updates from assigned through arrival and job completion. Chat in-app if you need to share details.

SECURE PAYMENT
Pay for your jump-start with Stripe in the app. Keep booking history and manage vehicles for faster next-time requests.

WHO IT’S FOR
Drivers and vehicle owners who need on-demand roadside jump-start assistance — not for service providers (use Ryvo Driver for that).
