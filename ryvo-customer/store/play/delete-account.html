Delete your Ryvo account

This page is for the Ryvo rider app on Google Play, published by Ryvo Ltd.

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app. Email enquiries@ryvo.arcadexit.com from the email address on the account, with the subject “Delete my Ryvo account”, and include the phone number you used to sign in.

Ryvo deletes the account and the personal data tied to it, including your profile, saved places, vehicles, messages, and notification token. Trip and payment records may be kept where the law requires them, including tax, fraud prevention, and dispute handling.

If you still have the app, you can send the same request from Profile → Delete account. Spend any wallet balance first. A balance left on the account is closed with it.
